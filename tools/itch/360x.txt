360X (PlayStation)  -  version @VERSION@
=========================================

360X is a cave-flying shooter by NMS Software. BMG Interactive was due to
publish it in 1996, and it was never released. This disc is rebuilt from
NMS's own July 1996 PlayStation source, which lead programmer Paul Griffiths
kept and the PlayStation Museum preserved.

What NMS left unfinished was completed from their own PC and Saturn versions
of the game. Where no version had something, the game says so (touching a
survivor shows "RESCUE NOT AVAILABLE": no version ever implemented rescues).
Every change is listed in the restoration's README.

To play:
  - Boot 360x.cue in a PS1 emulator (DuckStation, PCSX-Redux, PSoXide), or
  - burn it to a CD-R and play it on a console that runs CD-Rs.
  Pick a mission on the planet select with Left and Right. High scores save
  to a memory card.
  Controls (type A): Left/Right turn, Cross thrust, Square or Circle fire,
  Triangle grab a pod, R2 brake, L1/R1 change weapon, Start pause, Select
  menu. Pausing shows them on screen.

Credits (from the game's own credits screen, Saturn version, 1996):
